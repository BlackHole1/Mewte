# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Mewte to be a project where anyone can take part without being harassed, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation or socioeconomic status.

This document follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Where this text is silent, that document is our reference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intentions, and ask before you assume the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person. Explain your reasoning.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and admit mistakes when they happen.
- Use welcoming, inclusive language. Many of us are not writing in our first language.
- Put the health of the community ahead of winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their explicit permission.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking or repeated contact after being asked to stop.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review and any other channel the project uses. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer through the contact details on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/BlackHole1). Please do not open a public issue for this.

Describe what happened, where, and include links or screenshots if you can. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainers review every report and respond in proportion to the harm done:

1. **Correction**: a private note explaining what was inappropriate and why.
2. **Warning**: a clear warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban**: a ban from interacting with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban**: removal from the project's spaces.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code. Maintainers are held to the same standard as everyone else.
